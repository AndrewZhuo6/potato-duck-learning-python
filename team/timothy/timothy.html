// About me

My name is Timothy Pardin, but my friends call me Tim. I'm an Indonesian student in my second year of Artificial Intelligence at Beijing Institute of Technology. I moved here in 2025 and I'm still not used to the winter.

My interests sits across software engineering, data management, and human-centric psychology. What connects them for me is behavioural research also studying how people actually think and work, then using that to shape how a system talks to them. That's what pulled me toward context engineering. I still have a lot to learn in all three, and that's mostly the point.

// My role

In our team, Potato Duck, I'm the Project Manager/CEO. I decide what we build and when we build it. I write down what each page should do, keep our task list on GitHub, and check that everyone knows what they're working on this week. I also help and contribute to the code, but I mostly make sure the code we write is the code we actually need.

I've done this before as a Scrum project manager during an internship in Jakarta, and as team lead on a small game called Aisling.
